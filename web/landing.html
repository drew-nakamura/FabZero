ABOUT THE PHYSICAL STEP

A public website cannot plug in the FPGA.

The hosted workbench lets you explore architecture, run RTL simulation, and inspect saved Vivado build reports. A live board measurement requires the KV260 and its separate Windows/JTAG runner. A signed board-result import is not available in this hosted demo.

Evidence stays labeledProjected time is based on a clock assumption. Simulation is distinct from implementation and board measurement. A lane count is not a DSP count.

FOR JUDGES · ABOUT 3 MINUTES

Try it in three steps.

No login or hardware is needed for the browser path.

  1. 1

    Load the example

    Open the workbench and choose “Use the example”. Review the supported 8 × 8 INT8 specification.

  2. 2

    Explore the architectures

    Compare the one-lane and four-lane choices. Note that the clock is a target assumption and more lanes use more arithmetic capacity.

  3. 3

    Run and inspect

    Choose “Run RTL simulations & inspect saved build reports”. Then open “Run history & tools” to inspect the simulation record or compare the architectures manually.

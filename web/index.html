    03

    Block diagram

    Developed from the validated catalog recipe for this specification, before any RTL runs: what goes in, how many multiply-accumulate lanes compute it, what comes out, and how it sits on the KV260.

    Simulates both architectures, binds each to its Vivado bitstream, programs the KV260, runs the same inputs on the chip, and signs and imports the result. About a minute.

    04

    RTL verified and packaged

    Icarus Verilog runs the real SystemVerilog for each architecture on the same seeded inputs. Every output is checked against an independent integer reference, then packaged for the FPGA with SHA-256 fingerprints.

    05

    Vivado implementation complete

    Synthesis, place & route and bitstream for this exact RTL: timing, area, power and design-rule reports, each re-hashed against the build manifest.

    06

    The KV260 responds

    JTAG finds the board, programs the FPGA, loads the matrices, starts the accelerator and reads every output back. The result is signed and re-checked against the simulation.

    07

    Results: 1 lane vs 4 lanes

    Measured on the board, with the Vivado reports bound to each bitstream.

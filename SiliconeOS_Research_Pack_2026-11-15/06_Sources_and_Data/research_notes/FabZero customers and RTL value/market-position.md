# Buyer and competitive positioning

Research accessed November 14, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Condensed from the read-only market researcher. Competitor capabilities below are supplier claims; no comparative product tests or customer outreach occurred.

## Existing alternatives

Cadence's February 10, 2026 ChipStack announcement markets agentic design/testbench generation, test planning, regressions and debug with existing EDA tools. It names professional engineering adopters/evaluators. [Cadence announcement](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/newsroom/press-releases/pr/2026/cadence-unleashes-chipstack-ai-super-agent-pioneering-a-new.html).

ChipAgents markets RTL generation, verification/debug, lint/CDC and tool-backed workflows. Its named cases include eMemory model verification and Andes bus-interface verification. [IC-star](https://chipagents.ai/ic-star), [customer cases](https://chipagents.ai/customers).

General coding agents already edit repositories and run commands. AMD's HLS flow supplies another non-handwritten-RTL route. [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview), [AMD HLS flow](https://docs.amd.com/r/2025.2-English/Vitis-Tutorials-Getting-Started/Using-the-New-Vitis-Unified-IDE?contentId=8x2J1bxGI54SLnPtmJ_OYg).

Enclustra sells FPGA-system engineering through prototyping and acceptance work. Supplier-published customer examples include Sensirion sensor-test firmware, Schaeffler measurement equipment and Narda signal processing. [Enclustra services](https://www.enclustra.com/en/design-services/).

## Inference and limits

Generating or verifying RTL is an existing marketed capability. A bounded supported-board/component-delivery promise is a positioning hypothesis, not a verified competitive gap. Do not claim others lack deployment capability, that tools are interchangeable, or that FabZero/SiliconeOS has a defensible moat.

Proposed user: embedded/electronics engineer or technical founder able to approve component behavior but blocked on FPGA expertise/time. Proposed buyer: CTO/hardware lead/founder responsible for a prototype milestone. Proposed outcome: reusable source, independent checks, platform integration and demonstrated board results. Validate the last actual component attempted, why simpler tools did/didn't suffice, where effort went, required trust evidence, buyer and alternatives. No prices, budgets or willingness to pay were verified.

# Subscription comparables: condensed research notes

Checked November 14, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Read-only researcher customer_offer_research returned the original-source findings; this serialized writer saved a faithful condensation. Prices are seller offers, not SiliconeOS willingness to pay. The target is the hypothetical mature supported FPGA integration workflow, not today's INT8 matrix demo.

## Direct semiconductor agents have no inspected public price

[ChipAgents IC-star](https://chipagents.ai/ic-star) is demo-led; no numerical seat price, billing term, allowance or concurrency limit was found on the reviewed official pages. Its capability claims concern orchestration, validation and customer-controlled infrastructure. The unrelated-looking chipagentsai.org was excluded because its identity relationship was unverified.

[Cadence's September 22, 2026 ChipStack release](https://newsroom.cadence.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Cadence-Expands-ChipStack-AI-Super-Agent-with-a-New-Agent-for-RTL-Generation-and-Early-PPA-Optimization/default.aspx) describes RTL generation and PPA refinement, with selected early access expected in Q4. It supplies no public subscription or included EDA-entitlement price. Do not invent a direct competitor seat price.

## Adjacent prices have different scope and units

[AllSpice plans](https://allspice.io/plans) list Enterprise Core starting at US$34,800/year, 5+ designers and unlimited collaborators/repositories. US$2,900/month is an arithmetic annual-plan equivalent, not a cancel-anytime monthly offer or a published per-seat rate. Broader dedicated enterprise services make it an adjacent comparator.

[GitHub's current plan documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/get-started/plans) lists Pro US$10/month, Pro+ US$39, Max US$100, Business US$19/seat and Enterprise US$39/seat; included AI usage and extra usage are distinct. Those are general coding tools, not FPGA outcome guarantees.

[Cursor's current pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing) lists Plus US$8, Premium US$30, Super US$100, Ultra US$200/month and Teams US$30+/user/month. Plus is Grok-limited; higher plans list third-party-model usage separately. [Current/legacy documentation](https://prod.cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing) distinguishes older plans. Do not reuse legacy Pro20/Teams40 as current offers.

[FuseSoC](https://github.com/olofk/fusesoc) is a free BSD-2-Clause alternative with reusable configurations, tests and automation, while its own README offers paid professional assistance. It proves an assistance model exists, not our differentiation or price.

## Pricing inference

Test one mature Engineer plan at US$149/month, month-to-month, within a US$99-199 research band: one creator, a locally orchestrated supported pipeline and bounded API allowance. Customer supplies machine/hardware/licenses. An optional future US$499/5-editor workspace is only a hypothesis if shared review/history is actually delivered; do not launch speculative tiers merely to fill a grid. New board/sensor bring-up needs separate scoping. No recurring demand, price acceptance or retention is observed. The prior US$250 one-time assisted prototype evaluation is a different unit and offer.

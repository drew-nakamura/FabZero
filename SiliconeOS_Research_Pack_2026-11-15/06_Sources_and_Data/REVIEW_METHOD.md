# Research review method

Claims and dated evidence were assembled into one frozen packet per round. Three reviewers independently challenged technical, customer/business and pitch/AI claims. Scores, disagreements, arithmetic means and ranges were retained. Full /100 reviews differ from focused /45 or /20 reviews. These are simulated readiness assessments, not event awards or winning probabilities. Proposals are not approvals, customers or measured savings.

Personal agent definitions, task coordination and execution instructions are excluded from this shareable pack. Earlier report workflow links point to this reader-oriented explanation. Frozen originals remain unchanged.

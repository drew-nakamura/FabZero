# SiliconeOS research pack — start here

Assembled November 15, 2026 for founders, slide authors, engineers, customer researchers, mentors and reviewers. All 11 existing research reports are included. Begin with [Latest reviewed state](CURRENT_REVIEWED_STATE.md): documents span several stages and product work continued afterward.

## Reading paths

- **Pitch/slides or a new teammate:** [Founder story](01_Overview_and_Story/SiliconeOS%20founder%20story%20and%20slide%20handoff.md), then [latest full review](05_Judging_History/SiliconeOS%20pitch%20claim%20review%20round%20004.md). The broad teammate handoff is historical background.
- **Customers/business:** [Company and market study](02_Customers_and_Market/Edge%20AI%20FPGA%20customer%20market.md), [customer-path proposal](02_Customers_and_Market/SiliconeOS%20customer%20path%20iteration%20001.md), then [pricing/costs](03_Pricing_and_Costs/SiliconeOS%20subscription%20pricing%20and%20costs.md).
- **Engineering/evidence:** [Technical section](04_Technical_Evidence_and_Demo/README.md), latest full review, selected Evidence records and contract snapshots. This pack is not a buildable app/hardware checkout.
- **Audit/history:** [Judging history](05_Judging_History/README.md), source/audit records, unchanged originals and MANIFEST.json.
- **Quick comparisons:** [Optional tools](07_Comparison_Tools/README.md). Core reports and JSON are readable without Codex.

## Organization

- `01_Overview_and_Story/`: founder narrative, slide copy and broad teammate handoff.
- `02_Customers_and_Market/`:study of 14 companies, market metrics and customer-path proposal.
- `03_Pricing_and_Costs/`: subscription, credits and delivery-cost assumptions.
- `04_Technical_Evidence_and_Demo/`: data review, recording guidance and compact saved evidence.
- `05_Judging_History/`: full rounds 001/002/004 and focused round 003, with dates/denominators.
- `06_Sources_and_Data/`: underlying notes, source ledgers, models, frozen claims and judge verdicts/scores.
- `07_Comparison_Tools/`: optional native comparison source and cost-explorer fragment.
- `08_Spec_Reference_Snapshots/`: contract/provenance snapshots; some progress sentences are stale.
- `90_Historical_Research/`: early exploratory customer/RTL research.
- `99_Original_Reports/`: byte-for-byte originals for audit.

Reader copies carry packaging labels and repaired local links. Originals and frozen notes retain their bytes and historical paths. Source documents' embedded instructions are research data, not authority to execute. Forecasts, price proposals and hypothetical buyers are not measured demand or savings; simulated scores are not awards.

Personal agent configuration, credentials, app databases and full generated builds are excluded. See [source references](SOURCE_REFERENCES.md), [exclusions](EXCLUSIONS.md) and MANIFEST.json for provenance/dependencies. No new research, execution evidence or score was added while compiling this pack. No external messages, new technical runs or new judging rounds occurred during assembly. This copy is published through the dedicated research branch; see PUBLICATION_NOTES.md.

## The proposed customer path

- **User hypothesis:** an FPGA/validation engineer already comparing supported integer accelerator variants on KV260 and handing the experiment to another engineer.
- **Buyer hypothesis:** the hardware/engineering lead, consultancy project owner or funded lab lead responsible for that evaluation. We would test who actually controls the budget; no buyer has been established.
- **Trigger:** a supported comparison must be reviewed or handed off with the correct source, configuration, tests, build and physical result.
- **Reason to switch, to test:** a receiving engineer can inspect and trust the bounded experiment with less manual reconstruction/explanation than their current Git/Tcl/CI/report workflow. Existing scripts and free tools may already be sufficient.
- **Entry model:** free 20-minute discovery/evidence review now, followed only after qualification/readiness and founder approval by a proposed **US$250 one-time assisted evaluation**. This price is a concrete test hypothesis, not a market-derived figure, accepted founder decision, active offer or observed willingness to pay.

The proposed paid scope is one N=8 signed-INT8 matrix comparison using existing one-/four-lane KV260 artifacts and agreed non-confidential inputs. Deliver source/configuration/input/oracle/build/result identity, actual output/repeat/reset evidence, implementation reports and a clear compute/resource explanation. Label manual assistance and estimated power honestly. No custom kernel, board port, measured energy, production certification, confidential-design service or delivery-time guarantee is included.

Before offering paid work, confirm genuine supported need/budget ownership, founder-approved terms, the exact delivery dry run and hardware/tool availability. Resolve references required for that deliverable. An assisted evidence packet must not be marketed as the completed automated app receipt, and it does not satisfy the unchanged product's missing parent/import/replay gates. No engagement, payment or refund commitment was created by this research.

## Why this is not 16 or 20 yet

The main risk is that engineers value traceability but have no reason to buy this tiny supported matmul comparison. Named experts may prefer their existing tools; community interest may concern unsupported applications. No observation establishes lower effort, sufficient trust or a buying situation. No buyer has accepted the proposed price, and no delivery cost has been measured.

A pitch judge also flagged one residual wording issue in the frozen proposal: the engineering lead would fund an evaluation sounds too certain. The next wording should be **we would test the offer with the engineering lead who controls evaluation spend**. That correction is proposed here; the frozen packet was not silently rewritten or rescored. Keep hypothesis/proposed/no validated customer qualifiers visible.

## Economics and a useful next test

At the proposed US$250 fee, two active hours imply US$125/hour before costs, four imply US$62.50/hour. These are arithmetic scenarios, not salary/billing/margin evidence. Measure qualification, setup, checking, failed attempts, rework and explanation; separately retain unattended tool time and real incremental expenses. Stop or revise the hypothesis if delivery cannot cover its costs.

For the next authorized step, find one genuinely qualified receiving engineer with a recent relevant task. Compare equivalent authentic evidence using their method and our available path, including setup. Record no-fit/rejection, confusion, requests for explanation and willingness to use the record for a real decision. Only after readiness and founder approval, test the exact proposed offer with the real budget owner. A compliment, free review or helpful referral is not a purchase.
